Privacy policy

CricketCanClubs website and apps (iPhone and Android) · Last updated 27 October 2026

CricketCanClubs helps community cricket clubs in Canada run their season: live scores, fixtures, scorecards, rankings and player availability. This page explains what we collect, why, who can see it, and how to delete it.

What we collect

  • Your account: name, mobile number and/or email address, password (stored scrambled, never readable), your club and your role (player, captain, club admin).
  • Your player profile (optional): full name, gender, age, playing role, batting and bowling style, and any other details you choose to add.
  • Cricket activity: matches you play, runs, wickets, catches, availability for matches and the playing XI.
  • Scorecard photos that captains and club admins upload, and the scores read from them.
  • Messages to the AI Assistant and basic usage records (for example sign-ins and changes made) to keep the service safe and fix problems.

We do not collect your location, contacts or payment details, and we do not use advertising or tracking cookies. The apps only use the camera or photo library when you choose to upload a scorecard photo.

How we use it

  • To run your club's matches, scorecards, statistics and rankings.
  • To let captains see who is available and pick the team.
  • To read paper scorecard photos automatically and answer AI Assistant questions (see "Services we use").
  • To send you messages about your club inside the app.

Who can see what

  • Public (anyone): club names, fixtures, live scores, published scorecards and rankings, including players' names and cricket statistics.
  • Never public: your mobile number, email address and age.
  • Your club's captains and admins can see members' contact details and availability to run the club.
  • We do not sell or rent your information to anyone.

Services we use

  • Microsoft Azure hosts the website and stores the data.
  • Google Gemini (AI): scorecard photos and AI Assistant messages are sent to Google's Gemini service to read the scores and write answers. Only what is needed for that request is sent.

How long we keep it

While your account is open. Published scorecards and match statistics are part of the club's match history and stay after an account is deleted.

Delete your account

Signed in, open Profile and tap Delete my account (type DELETE to confirm). This removes your sign-in account and your settings at once and signs you out on every device. Your name in published scorecards stays as part of the club's match records; to have it removed or changed, ask your club admin or contact us.

Children

Junior players' accounts should be set up by a parent, guardian or club admin. Contact us to remove a child's information.
